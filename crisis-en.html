Safety and Crisis Situations

Published August 12, 2026

This page describes what the Lights On Me application (the "App") does when acute distress is expressed while using it: how it watches over you, what is shown, what is recorded, and the design commitments that govern this system.

If you are going through a difficult moment as you read this:

  • To talk to someone in your language and in your country: findahelpline.com
  • 988: crisis and listening line, for North America (call or text)
  • If you are in immediate danger: 112 for Europe, 911 for North America, or your local emergency number

1. Nature and Limits of the Service

Lights On Me is a voice coach for personal growth. Lights On Me is not a medical, psychological or therapeutic service. It does not in any way replace a consultation with a healthcare professional, and it is not an emergency service or a suicide prevention system.

We believe, however, that an app which invites people to open up carries a responsibility: not to stay passive when acute distress is expressed. The system described here is a safety net, not care.

2. Detection of Acute Distress Signals

Only if you have explicitly consented to it in the App (a separate consent, withdrawable at any time from Settings), the App analyses the text of what you share (transcriptions of your voice sessions, text you type) in order to detect signals of acute distress: suicide or self-harm risk, abuse suffered, addiction, eating disorders.

The system combines several independent layers, designed so that detection remains available even if one of them fails. We do not publish its technical details: describing precisely what triggers detection would make it easier to circumvent, which would weaken the protection for everyone.

3. Upon Detection

The session pauses gently: instead of coach feedback, the App immediately shows a support message and verified help resources, the ones listed at the top of this page. The message says this: what you are sharing deserves a human voice, trained to listen without judgment.

A detection does not block your account, does not remove any feature and carries no judgment. No external human contact is triggered automatically. You remain the sole decision-maker on what happens next.

4. Data Recorded

Each detection is logged for safety purposes, in minimised form: the log does not record the topic detected (it only notes whether the signal indicated a suicide risk or another form of distress) and has contained no excerpt of your words since July 27, 2026. The full details of this log, its retention periods and your rights are described in our Privacy Policy, which prevails.

5. Design Commitments

  • Detection stays active in all circumstances. It is not subject to the technical or budget ceilings that apply to the App's other functions.
  • It is based on what you express. No questionnaire score, no biometric data, no acoustic analysis of your voice: only your words count.
  • No diagnosis. The App does not diagnose, does not offer treatment and does not assess your state of health.
  • A framework designed not to create dependence. The coach is trained to encourage real-life connections and autonomy, without fostering attachment or making you feel guilty for an absence.
  • A service for adults. The App is restricted to adults (18 and over).
